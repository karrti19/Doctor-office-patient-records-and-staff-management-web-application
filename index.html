TEAM ADMINISTRATION

Staff & access

A proposed role model for the future secured application.

✚
CLINICAL ROLE

Doctor

Responsible for clinical care and medical documentation.

  • View patient records
  • Create and update visit notes
  • Record diagnoses and prescriptions
  • Review patient history
Permissions must be enforced by the backend.
▤
FRONT DESK ROLE

Receptionist

Responsible for registration and administrative workflows.

  • Register patients
  • Update contact details
  • Manage appointments and check-ins
  • View only information needed for their work
Clinical notes and prescriptions should be restricted as appropriate.

Before adding staff login

Use a trusted authentication service or secure backend. Do not store passwords in this website's JavaScript, browser storage, or repository. The role labels on this page are planning guidance, not functioning security.
